# Plastic Surgeon | Free Plastic Surgeon Website Template

A Framer template for independent plastic and aesthetic surgery clinics that want to look careful, not clinical.

## The idea

Every elective surgery starts with a documented sitting: the same chair, the same light, the same angles. Plastic Surgeon is built around that moment. Photography leads, prices are plain, and recovery is shown honestly, week by week.

## The contact sheet

Each case is a four-frame contact sheet labeled Week 0, 1, 6 and 12. Visitors select a frame and the caption explains what that week really looks like, from swelling and stitches to returning to work. It works with a mouse, a keyboard or a thumb, and it respects reduced-motion settings.

## Built for privacy

Consults are camera-off by default. The booking form asks only for what a clinic needs to reply, and every case is marked as shown with consent.

## What is included

- Ten pages: Home, Procedures, Results, Surgeon, Pricing, Guides, Book, Imprint, 404 and Styleguide
- CMS collections for procedures, cases, guides, patient notes and FAQ
- Procedure pages with starting price, downtime and who it suits
- Contact sheet code component bound to the Cases collection
- Booking form with helper, error and success states
- Desktop, tablet and phone layouts with a phone-only consult strip
- Source Serif 4 and Outfit, both free on Google Fonts
- A cool, daylit palette with one accent, set up as colour styles

## Who it is for

Independent surgeons, small aesthetic practices and clinic groups that want a site patients can read calmly, with prices and recovery times they can plan around.
